Vice Cover Studio turns your photos and a caption into multi-panel GTA-style cover art — 1–4 panel layouts, four colour grades (Vice Sunset, Neon Night, Golden Hour, Miami Teal), bold Anton lettering — and exports it as a PNG in 1080 × 1080, 1080 × 1350 or 1920 × 1080. It runs offline from static files; your photos never leave your machine. No Rockstar artwork, logos or fonts: the lettering is the SIL Open Font License Anton face, vendored under `app/fonts/`.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# open http://localhost:8080
```

Drop in your photos (or tap a sample), pick a layout and a grade, type your caption, drag a panel photo to crop it, and hit **Export PNG**.
